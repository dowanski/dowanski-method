# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — Adaptive discovery / September 9, 2026

### What changed

- Added A–D collaboration choices, separate from question delivery and authority.
- Replaced next-uncovered-anchor routing with the next consequential discovery
  move; question wording is explicitly adaptable.
- Added Light Scan, Standard Research, and Deep Research recommendations with
  effort, tool-availability, evidence, and return boundaries.
- Research can resolve several anchors; routine findings are recorded without
  repeatedly asking permission to document them.
- Added explicit readiness recommendations and preservation of human gates.
- Made startup wording plainer and distinguished contained cleanup self-review
  from the independent resumption review still required for cycle/handoff gates.
- Kept all three DM foundations and the 96/14/26/34 download counts unchanged.

### Why and intended benefit

Owner review found the first conversation could feel generic and survey-like.
The revision is intended to make discovery more informed, collaborative, and
proportionate without turning every question into research. These are intended
benefits, not measured improvements in adoption, quality, speed, or token use.

### Verification and adoption

Ten local verification groups pass, including twenty-four injected faults.
Three isolated fictional-project conversations and focused repairs/retests
are documented; two blind cross-agent checks passed their declared resume routes.
One required its routed cleanup/Blueprint beyond the core five files. See the
[verification scope and limits](guides/verification.md). Written-contract checks
do not establish conversational quality. Published v0.2.0 remains available.
Do not overwrite an active project's populated records or silently upgrade it.
Review changed instructions, preserve accepted context, and choose deliberately.

## 0.2.0 — Initial public edition / September 9, 2026

- Combined QDI discovery and inactive Light, Standard, and Governed receivers
  into one default starter, with separate advanced foundation packages.
- Added explicit phase, sidequest-return, cleanup, handoff, and human gates.
- Reduced required startup reading while retaining continuing authority,
  quality, review, and context/usage controls across the QDI-to-DM transition.
- Repaired selected navigation paths and isolated maintainer/adopter entrypoints.
- Aligned license notices with the combined folder structure.
- Added a public-facing source introduction and local packaging/verification.

Earlier development candidates were private. This file does not imply they
were published releases. The version authority is `starter/VERSION`.
