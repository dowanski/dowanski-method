# Brand and Identity Boundary

“The Dowanski Method” identifies the source framework and its canonical
releases. The open licenses permit use of the material they cover; they do not
grant permission to:

- impersonate Dowanski;
- imply endorsement, certification, partnership, or official status;
- use a Dowanski logo or custom brand artwork as the identity of a derivative;
- present a modified system as an unchanged official Dowanski Method release.

Accurate descriptions include “based on,” “adapted from,” or “uses a Dowanski
Method template.” Only unmodified releases from the canonical Dowanski source
should be called official releases.
