## Ledger rules

- Preserve how understanding developed without turning every chat sentence into
  documentation.
- Record material answers, corrections, evidence, assumptions, contradictions,
  decisions, branches, and return points.
- Use the classifications below; read only the relevant entries when updating.
- Quote the human only when exact wording is decision-relevant; otherwise use a
  faithful summary and invite correction.
- Never place credentials, secrets, or unnecessary sensitive personal or
  customer information in this file.
- A correction supersedes the active interpretation but does not erase useful
  provenance.

## Evidence classifications

- `OWNER_STATEMENT` — the human's reported experience, intent, or decision.
- `OBSERVED` — directly inspected current state.
- `SOURCED` — supported by a named external or internal source.
- `INFERENCE` — reasoned interpretation not yet confirmed.
- `ASSUMPTION` — relied upon without sufficient evidence.
- `UNKNOWN` — consequential information not yet known.
- `CONTRADICTION` — statements or evidence that cannot yet coexist.
- `DEFERRED` — unresolved deliberately, with an owner and return gate.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` — excluded with a recorded reason.
- `OWNER_CONFIRMED` — a synthesis or decision explicitly accepted by the human.

Do not silently upgrade a statement, inference, or assumption into verified
fact. Owner confirmation establishes accepted direction; external claims still
need their own evidence.
