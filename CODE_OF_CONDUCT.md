# Community Conduct

The Dowanski Method welcomes specific, good-faith feedback about documentation,
templates, accessibility, safety boundaries, and real use cases.

Participants are expected to:

- address the work rather than attack the person;
- describe observations honestly and separate them from assumptions;
- protect private, customer, employer, and security-sensitive information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, impersonation, or deceptive
  claims of endorsement;
- respect a maintainer's decision to narrow, defer, or close a discussion.

Public issues may be edited, locked, or removed when they expose sensitive
material, target people, repeat spam, or prevent constructive review. Serious
security concerns belong in the private route described in `SECURITY.md`.

This repository is owner-maintained. Participation does not guarantee that a
proposal will be accepted, scheduled, or included in an official release.
